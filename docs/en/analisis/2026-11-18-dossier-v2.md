<h2>Techniques in detail</h2>

<h3>Implicit Urgency – The Objective</h3>
<p>Real text: "Even so, the most requested numbers usually sell out before December, so making the purchase early is a prudent option."<br>
Detection: A sense is created that the reader must act immediately to avoid missing an opportunity, even though a specific deadline is not explicitly mentioned.</p>

<h3>Appealing to Fear and Sexual Insecurity – ABC</h3>
<p>Real text: "Fatigue and stress constitute some of the main enemies of sexual relations."<br>
Detection: It appeals to the audience's fear regarding their intimate performance, suggesting that the product or proposed action can alleviate that fear.</p>

<h3>Name Calling – elDiario.es</h3>
<p>Real text: "Deutsche Bank financed the operations of the blacklisted Spanish companies investigated for corruption."<br>
Detection: The entity is labeled with a pejorative term ("blacklist"), generating an emotional reaction without offering concrete data.</p>

<h3>Fear (alarm) – La Vanguardia</h3>
<p>Real text: "The internal communications network of the National Security Agency (NSA) goes down."<br>
Detection: A security threat is emphasized without delving into the cause, fueling the reader's anxiety.</p>

<h3>Card Stacking – 20 Minutos</h3>
<p>Real text: "The creation of the immigrant center in Rwanda opens the EU's 'deterrence policy' and completely corners Sánchez's thesis."<br>
Detection: Only arguments favoring the media outlet's position are presented, discarding any contrary point of view.</p>
