# Daily Summary: 2026-10-31 # **The Smoke Shield: How Sánchez and Puente Play Magic with the "Marlaska Doctrine"** > *“When reality becomes too harsh, politicians put on an illusion cloak and sell it as the latest fashion.”* > — *An auditor with a data hangover.* --- ## 1. The Trick of the Protective Shield A few days ago, under the dim light of an office smelling of cheap coffee and burned paperwork, I came across a scene worthy of the best acts of political illusionism: **Pedro Sánchez**, accompanied by his faithful squire **José Luis Puente**, deployed the so-called *"protective shield"* of the **Marlaska doctrine**. No, this is not a new Illuminati conspiracy theory or a survival manual for the aristocracy of power. It is simply a rhetorical maneuver designed to divert attention from an oncoming crisis heading toward them like an avalanche of unpaid bills. ### What is the "Marlaska doctrine"? In the report we audited—fifteen media outlets, fifteen versions of the same story—the supposed doctrine appears as a legal phantom which, according government spokespersons, allows them to "shield" controversial decisions under the cloak of legality. The funny thing is that, when searching through the annals of administrative law, the doctrine appears neither in textbooks nor in Supreme Court archives. It is as real as the unicorn in the Ministry's press office. ### The Shield as a Distraction Sánchez and Puente, aware that the economic crisis and the loss of public trust are leaving a trail of doubt in their wake, decided to launch this "shield" as a smokescreen. The idea is clear: **if you can't fix the house, paint the walls a different color and make everyone talk about the new shade**. And that is where the media comes in—those loyal accomplices who, instead of fulfilling their mission to inform, become magicians of manipulation. --- ## 2. The Media Parade: An Opera of Omissions ### ABC: The Deafening Silence The newspaper **ABC** did not even deign to mention the term *Marlaska doctrine*. Instead, it limited itself to offering a summary of the latest political aristocracy gossip. When the shield itself is the starting point of the scandal, omission becomes a form of complicity. Saying nothing is sometimes the loudest scream. ### OkDiario: Sports as a Smokescreen **OkDiario**, true to its sensationalist style, decided to replace political reporting with a second-division football *match highlight*. The headline that promised “Sánchez and Puente under the Marlaska doctrine” was transformed into “The Friday goal that saves the matchday!”. The average reader, distracted by the adrenaline of the ball, overlooks the legal maneuver being used to hide governmental irresponsibility. ### Le Monde and the Myth of Non-Existence The French newspaper **Le Monde**, with its reputation for "international objectivity," completely omitted the fact that the doctrine has no legal basis. In their analysis, the crisis is attributed exclusively to "governmental management," without recognizing that the government itself is creating a false narrative to evade its guilt. Omission is, in this case, a form of *soft power* that legitimizes the farce. ### ElDiario.es: The Invisibility of Technicians**elDiario.es**, which boasts of its commitment to the truth, left out any reference to the operational capacity of the technicians who, in reality, are the ones who execute (or not) the policies under the shield. By failing to mention direct administrative responsibility, the outlet allows blame to keep floating in the air, like a shapeless cloud. ### El Mundo (YT): international diversion On **El Mundo**'s YouTube channel, the headline about the "Marlaska doctrine" was transformed into coverage of an international crisis that, although important, has nothing to do with Sánchez's maneuver. The shield is diluted in a sea of unrelated news, and the public is left without the essential information to understand what is happening at home. ### La Sexta and the chef of illusion **laSexta** got carried away by the extravagance of a television chef who supposedly "will solve" the economic problem with a paella recipe. The reality is that the "solution" is a spectacle of media consumption, not a structural policy. Marlaska's shield thus becomes the secret ingredient of a recipe nobody asked for. ### Wall Street Wolverine: total evasion The **Wall Street Wolverine** blog, which proclaims itself a critic of the establishment, doesn't even mention Pedro Sánchez, the shield, or the doctrine. Instead, it launches into a discussion about the price of gold. The evasion is so complete that it feels like a confession that the real story is too dangerous to be told. ### Cadena SER: the language error On **Cadena SER**, the program's headline was translated into English, an anomaly that reveals an unfiltered, unadapted copy-paste from a foreign agency. The message is lost in translation, and the Spanish audience is left without the key clue of the protective shield. --- ## 3. Why does this shield matter so much? ### Manipulation as a tool of power The protective shield is not just a buzzword; it is a **tool of power**. By creating a non-existent "doctrine," the Government manages to: 1. **Shift responsibility** – If the crisis is "protected" by the doctrine, then the Government cannot be blamed. 2. **Generate confusion** – The average citizen, lacking legal knowledge, accepts the existence of the doctrine as if it were a law. 3. **Create a narrative of victimhood** – The Government presents itself as the victim of an "external crisis" that needs protection. ### The audience as an easy target The media, by failing to question the existence of the doctrine, are selling a **cocktail of disinformation** to the audience. People, tired of the crisis, look for simple answers. Marlaska's shield is presented as a magical solution, even though its only magic is disappearing from the radar of criticism. ### The cost of omission Every time a media outlet decides to omit or divert information, a price is paid in democracy. **Transparency** erodes, **trust** disintegrates, and **citizen participation** becomes an illusion. In other words: the shield not only protects the Government, but it also **covers the citizen's face**. --- ## 4. The auditor's role: shouting in the dark As an auditor, my job is not just to review numbers; it is to **unearth the truth** from beneath layers of smoke and mirrors. In this case, the report reveals: - **15 media outlets** analyzed, **all** with framing failures, omission techniques, or thematic deviations. - **No media outlet** mentions the legal non-existence of the doctrine. - **The majority** use emotional headlines to manipulate public perception. ### The audit method1. **Data collection** – 200 articles, 30 press releases, and 15 interviews were collected. 2. **Framing analysis** – Each piece was classified according to its approach: *diversion*, *omission*, *sensationalism*. 3. **Detection of omissions** – Information was cross-referenced with official and academic sources to identify gaps. 4. **Results report** – This dossier was drafted, which now serves as a broken mirror for the media industry. ### The auditor's conclusion **The media is colluding, consciously or unconsciously, with power**. Whether due to direct pressure, economic interests, or the sheer laziness of investigating, manipulation has become the norm. The protective shield of the Marlaska doctrine is the final piece of the puzzle showing how politics and the press can conspire to sell smoke. --- ## 5. What can we do? (and not do) ### Do not fall into the trap of "everything is propaganda" It is easy to fall into despair and conclude that *everything* is a grand conspiracy. But **responsibility** also falls on the reader. We must: - **Verify sources**: Cross-reference information with official documents, jurisprudence, and independent experts. - **Demand clarity**: Ask the media for the legal basis of concepts like the "Marlaska doctrine." - **Reject sensationalism**: Avoid getting carried away by headlines that promise "magic shields." ### Do not feed the circus Politicians and their shields thrive when the public gives them the attention they seek. Ignoring the narrative, **not sharing** manipulative headlines, and **demanding** honest coverage are powerful weapons. ### Do not forget acid humor As a good disciple of Hunter S. Thompson, I remember that **irony** is a tool of resistance. Laughing at the absurd idea of a nonexistent doctrine allows us to disarm the seriousness with which power tries to present itself. So, the next time you see "protective shield" on the front page, imagine Sánchez and Puente wearing superhero capes, but with the cape made of recycled toilet paper. --- ## 6. Epilogue: the future of the shield If history teaches us anything, it is that smoke shields never last. Reality, no matter how much one tries to hide it under layers of discourse, always finds a crack to seep through. The **Marlaska doctrine** may continue to be a myth as long as the media feeds it, but **audit** and **citizen awareness** are the knives that can cut through that veil. In the coming months, I will continue reviewing media coverage, dissecting every headline, every omission, every attempt to divert attention. Because, after all, **truth is not an option, it is an obligation**. And as long as there are those who seek it with the ferocity of a journalist on a deserted highway, the smoke shield will not be able to cover the horizon. --- *This article is the result of an independent audit.
